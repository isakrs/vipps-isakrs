Public-safe knowledge

  • The first playable signal is a conceptual payment event such as tapping or sending money to a person.
  • The experience must never expose real payment data, real user data, secrets, or sensitive internal details.
  • Product teams send the signal in and later receive governed outputs back through visible trusted destinations.
  • Flow modes should be visible in the scene, not only described in text.
